# DESeq2 - Basic Differential Expression

We are going to walk through a quick example of how to use DESeq2 to perform a basic differential expression analysis. We will do this by analyzing a dataset from a publication by [O'Meara et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4344930/) looking at cardiac myocyte regeneration. The experiment in question was a RNAseq performed at 4 timepoints with two replicates across the differentiation process looking at days post-natal (0, 4, 7 and adult mice).
